# Fertilizer Recommendations for Dedza District

## Summary

Verified fertilizer recommendations for Dedza District focus on balanced nutrient management using high-analysis compound fertilizers, timely top dressing, and soil improvement practices. Farmers are encouraged to combine mineral fertilizers with organic inputs to improve soil fertility and increase crop productivity.

---

# Recommended Fertilizer Types

## Basal Fertilizer

Apply compound fertilizer **23:21+4S with Zinc (Zn)** at planting or immediately before planting.

This fertilizer provides:

- Nitrogen (N)
- Phosphorus (P)
- Sulphur (S)
- Zinc (Zn)

These nutrients promote early crop establishment and healthy root development.

---

## Top Dressing

Apply one of the following fertilizers **3 to 6 weeks after crop emergence**:

- Urea
- Calcium Ammonium Nitrate (CAN)

Top dressing should coincide with adequate soil moisture to maximize nutrient uptake.

---

## Soil Amendments

In areas with acidic soils, especially around **Bembeke Extension Planning Area (EPA)**, apply **Dolomitic Lime** before planting.

Benefits include:

- Raising soil pH
- Improving nutrient availability
- Increasing fertilizer efficiency
- Promoting healthy root growth

---

# Fertilizer Application Best Practices

- Place basal fertilizer directly into the planting hole or in a band beside the seed.
- Avoid placing fertilizer directly on the seed.
- Apply top dressing when sufficient soil moisture is available.
- Delaying top dressing beyond the recommended period may reduce maize yields by more than 30%.

---

# Integrated Soil Fertility Management

For sustainable production, combine mineral fertilizers with organic soil improvement practices.

Recommended options include:

- Farmyard manure
- Compost
- Crop residue incorporation
- Crop rotation using legumes such as pigeon peas or groundnuts

These practices may reduce mineral fertilizer requirements by approximately 30–50% while improving long-term soil health.

---

# Key Messages for Farmers

- Use 23:21+4S with Zinc during planting.
- Apply Urea or CAN 3–6 weeks after crop emergence.
- Apply lime where soils are acidic.
- Combine fertilizers with manure or compost.
- Apply fertilizers at the correct time for best yields.
