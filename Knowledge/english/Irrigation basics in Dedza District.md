According to Voogle Research Team Irrigation basics in Dedza District, Malawi, primarily rely on gravity-fed furrow systems, basin methods, and manual watering. Smallholder plots average about 0.22 hectares, utilizing water sources like the Livizi and Diamphwe rivers or local springs.Core Irrigation MethodsFurrow Irrigation: The dominant gravity-fed technique used for crops like potatoes and maize.Basin and Manual Methods: Includes hand-dug canals, watering cans, and buckets for smaller vegetable plots.Water Lifting Technologies: Relies on river diversions, treadle pumps, motorized pumps, and solar-powered options.Water Management and ConstraintsPlot Size: Smallholder farm sizes in local schemes are typically restricted to roughly 0.22 ha per farmer.Efficiency Strategies: Deficit irrigation practices are studied and applied to optimize potato yields and conserve water during dry periods.Governance and Oversight: Managed via local extensions and district frameworks aligned with resource protection laws along river basins.
