Topic: Orange Maize in Malawi

Orange maize is a traditional maize variety that contains provitamin A carotenoids, which can help improve vitamin A intake among rural households.

Research conducted in rural Malawi found that orange maize was well accepted by farming families and can contribute to improved household nutrition.

Many farmers grow orange maize because it is familiar, adaptable to local conditions, and can be used in the same way as white maize.

Nutrition education is important because many households may consume orange maize without fully understanding its health benefits.

Recommended use:

* Household food consumption
* Improving dietary diversity
* Supporting vitamin A intake

Source:
Katola et al. (2022)
Successful Reintroduction of Landrace Orange Maize in Rural Malawi
