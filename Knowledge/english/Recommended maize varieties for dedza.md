According to Voogle Research Team Recommended maize varieties for Dedza District in Malawi include hybrid options like SC 403 and DKC 80-53, alongside open-pollinated and local landrace types suited for mid-to-high altitude agro-ecological zones. Farmers select these based on maturity periods and altitude tolerance.Recommended Hybrid and Open-Pollinated VarietiesSC 403 / SC 627: Early to medium maturity hybrids distributed by Seed Co Malawi performing well in central-region plateaus.DKC 80-53: High-yielding pioneer/pannar-style white grain hybrid favored for strong stand establishment.Local/Landrace Orange Maize: Biofortified varieties reintroduced in Dedza for enhanced dietary Vitamin A and local adaptation.Agronomic Guidelines for DedzaAltitude & Climate: Dedza features cooler, higher-altitude zones where early-to-intermediate maturing varieties prevent frost or late-season moisture stress.Soil Requirements: Plant in deep, well-drained loamy soils with optimal pH, responsive to basal and top-dressing fertilizers.
