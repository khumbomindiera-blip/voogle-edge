Malinga ndi Voogle Research Team

Malangizo a feteleza ku Dedza District amayang'ana kwambiri pa kugwiritsa ntchito feteleza wa 23:21+4S wokhala ndi Zinc, Urea pa top dressing, komanso laimu wa ulimi kuti achepetse acidity ya nthaka.

Mitundu ya Feteleza Yovomerezeka

Basal Application:
Gwiritsani ntchito feteleza wa 23:21+4S kapena ma blend omwe ali ndi Zinc ndi Sulfur musanabzale kapena nthawi yobzala.

Top Dressing:
Ikani Urea kapena CAN patatha masabata 3 mpaka 6 mbeu zitamera.

Soil Amendments:
M'madera a acidic monga Bembeke EPA, gwiritsani ntchito dolomitic lime kuti mukweze pH ya nthaka ndi kuthandiza mbeu kuyamwa zakudya.

Njira Zabwino Zogwiritsira Ntchito

Placement:
Ikani feteleza m’dzenje la mbeu kapena pafupi ndi mbeu.

Timing:
Top dressing iyenera kuchitika nthaka ikakhala ndi chinyezi chokwanira patatha masabata 3 mpaka 4 mutabzala.

Integration:
Phatikizani feteleza ndi manyowa a ziweto, phulusa, kapena kulima nyemba monga nandolo ndi mtedza kuti muchepetse kugwiritsa ntchito feteleza wa mankhwala.
