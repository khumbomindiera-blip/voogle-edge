Malinga ndi Voogle Research Team

Njira zazikulu za ulimi wothirira ku Dedza zimaphatikizapo furrow irrigation, basin irrigation, ndi kuthirira pamanja.

Njira Zothirira

Furrow Irrigation:
Njira yotchuka kwambiri yomwe imagwiritsa ntchito madzi oyenda m'mizere.

Basin Irrigation:
Imagwiritsidwa ntchito m'minda ya masamba ndi minda yaying'ono.

Manual Watering:
Kugwiritsa ntchito zitini kapena ndowa kuthirira.

Magwero a Madzi

Mitsinje monga Livizi ndi Diamphwe.

Akasupe am'deralo.

Mapampu a treadle, mapampu a mafuta ndi mapampu a solar.

Kasamalidwe ka Madzi

Gwiritsani ntchito madzi mosamala nthawi ya chilala.

Limbikitsani njira zochepetsera kutayika kwa madzi.
