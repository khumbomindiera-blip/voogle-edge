Mitundu ya chimanga yomwe imalimbikitsidwa ku Dedza ndi SC 403, SC 627, DKC 80-53 komanso Orange Maize.

Mitundu Yolimbikitsidwa

SC 403:
Chimanga chofulumira kukhwima chomwe chimachita bwino ku Dedza.

SC 627:
Chimanga chomwe chimapereka zokolola zabwino m'madera a pakati pa Malawi.

DKC 80-53:
Chimanga chopereka zokolola zambiri komanso chokhazikika.

Orange Maize:
Chimanga chokhala ndi Vitamin A chomwe chimathandiza pa thanzi la mabanja.

Malangizo

Gwiritsani ntchito nthaka yakuya komanso yosungira madzi bwino.

Ikani feteleza wa basal ndi top dressing moyenera.
