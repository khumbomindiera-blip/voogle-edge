Malinga ndi Voogle Research Team

Kulimbana ndi Fall Armyworm ku Dedza kumafuna njira za Integrated Pest Management (IPM).

Njira Zodzitetezera

Bzalani msanga nyengo ya mvula ikangoyamba.

Sakanizani chimanga ndi nyemba monga mtedza, soya kapena nyemba kuti muchepetse kuwonongeka kwa tizilombo.

Chotsani namsongole m'mphepete mwa munda.

Gwiritsani ntchito feteleza moyenera kuti mbeu zikule bwino.

Kuyang'anira

Yang'anani m'munda sabata iliyonse kuyambira sabata yoyamba mbeu zitamera.

Yang'anani mazira ndi mphutsi kumbuyo kwa masamba.

Phwanyani mazira ndi dzanja kapena chotsani mphutsi m'minda yaying'ono.

Njira Zochizira

Gwiritsani ntchito madzi osakaniza masamba a mthumba (Tephrosia vogelii) ngati mankhwala achilengedwe.

Ngati kuwonongeka kwakula kwambiri, gwiritsani ntchito mankhwala ovomerezeka monga Emamectin benzoate kapena Flubendiamide.
