# Voogle Edge Technical Report

## Problem

Millions of African farmers lack reliable internet connectivity, preventing them from accessing agricultural knowledge, market information, and early warning systems.

Growing up in Chikangawa, Mzimba, Malawi, I experienced firsthand how poor connectivity limits access to information and opportunities. While digital agriculture solutions exist, most depend on continuous internet access and cloud services.

## Solution

Voogle Edge is an offline-first multilingual AI platform that provides localized agricultural intelligence directly on low-cost computers.

The platform stores agricultural knowledge locally and uses on-device language models to answer questions without requiring internet connectivity.

## Design Decisions

* Offline-first architecture
* Local language support
* Lightweight language models
* Edge computing
* Community intelligence reporting
* Future SMS integration

## Constraints

* Rural connectivity limitations
* Low-cost hardware requirements
* Limited computing resources
* Power instability

## Technologies

* Python
* Flask
* Ollama
* Local language models
* HTML/CSS

## Impact

Voogle Edge enables farmers to access agricultural advice, report disruptions, and receive localized intelligence even when internet services are unavailable.
